About the Studio

Diamant Games is a mobile games and apps studio based in Berlin, Germany. We make puzzle and word games that are simple to pick up, satisfying to play, and beautifully designed, and we keep improving them long after launch.

Alongside our own titles, we put the same product thinking to work for other teams through our consulting practice.

Consulting
